Questions

Good to know

Is it really only for older women and younger men?

Yes. Women must be 35 or older, and men must be between 21 and 34. We check dates of birth against ID during verification.

Who can see my information?

Only our verification team. Your details go to another member only after you both agree to be introduced.

What does it cost?

Applying is free. If a membership fee applies, our team explains it on your intro call before you pay anything. We will never ask you to pay anyone other than the agency, and never through a personal number without you confirming it with us.

Do you guarantee I'll find someone?

No honest agency can. We promise careful verification and thoughtful introductions, and that we will keep working with you.

What if someone asks me for money?

Do not send it. Tell us straight away on WhatsApp. Requests for money are the quickest way to be removed from our service.

Can I use my existing Meet GH app account?

The Meet GH app is a separate, open community. This agency is a curated service with human introductions. You can apply here regardless of whether you use the app.

Ready to meet someone worth meeting?

Start with a private application, or send us a WhatsApp message first. We're happy to answer anything.
