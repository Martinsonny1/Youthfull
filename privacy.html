Privacy Policy

Written to follow Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Draft for review. Please have a Ghanaian lawyer review this and add your registered business name and Data Protection Commission registration before launch.

1. What we collect

When you apply: your name, WhatsApp number, optional email, date of birth, city, what you are looking for, a short description, and how you heard about us. We do not ask women for ID. Men in our network provide an ID and a live selfie so we can verify them.

2. Why we collect it

To confirm you meet our age requirement, to contact you, to suggest suitable introductions and to keep members safe. We rely on your consent, which you give when you submit the form and can withdraw at any time.

3. Who sees it

Only our authorised team. Another member sees your profile details only when you have agreed to an introduction. We do not sell your information. Our service providers (such as our database host and messaging apps) process data on our behalf.

4. Men's ID images

Men's ID images and selfies are used only to verify them and are deleted after verification is complete, unless needed to investigate a report. Another member never sees them.

5. Keeping and deleting

We keep applications for as long as needed to run the service. Rejected applications are deleted within 90 days. You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your information by messaging us on WhatsApp.

6. Security

Applications are stored in an access-controlled database that only signed-in administrators can read. No online service is perfectly secure, so please never share your passwords or financial details with anyone.

7. Adults only

This service is strictly for adults. Applications from anyone under 35 are not accepted.
