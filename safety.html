Safety

Every man is verified. You never have to be.

Trust is the whole service. This is how we protect it.

How we verify every man

  • A valid government ID, checked for name and date of birth
  • A live selfie matched to the ID photo
  • Age confirmed within 21 to 34
  • A personal screening chat with our team
  • ID images kept private and deleted once checking is complete

No fake profiles

  • Men cannot sign up on their own. Our team adds each one personally
  • No stolen or borrowed photos, because of the selfie match
  • No public profiles for anyone to browse or copy
  • Anyone caught lying is removed and not allowed back

How introductions work

  • You see a short profile only for men who have agreed
  • Both of you must say yes before numbers are shared
  • You can say no to anyone, no explanation needed
  • You can pause or leave at any time

Before you meet

  • Have a video call first
  • Meet in a public place, such as a restaurant or hotel lobby
  • Tell a friend where you're going and with whom
  • Arrange your own transport

Red flags: tell us at once

  • Requests for money, airtime, gifts or "emergencies"
  • Pressure to move off WhatsApp or to meet in private
  • Refusal to do a video call
  • Disrespectful or sexual messages you did not ask for
Report any concern by WhatsApp. We act on every report and remove men who break our rules. In an emergency, call the Ghana Police on 191 or the National Emergency number 112.
