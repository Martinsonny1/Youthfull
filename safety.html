Safety

Verified people. Safe first meetings.

Trust is the whole service. This is how we protect it.

How we verify

  • Date of birth checked against a government ID
  • Live selfie or short video call to confirm it is you
  • Duplicate and fake profiles rejected
  • ID images are kept private and deleted once verification is complete

How introductions work

  • You see a short profile only after we've confirmed you're interested
  • Both sides must agree before numbers are shared
  • You can say no to anyone, no explanation needed
  • You can pause or leave at any time

Before you meet

  • Have a video call first
  • Meet in a public place, such as a restaurant or hotel lobby
  • Tell a friend where you're going and with whom
  • Arrange your own transport

Red flags: tell us at once

  • Requests for money, airtime, gifts or "emergencies"
  • Pressure to move off WhatsApp or to meet in private
  • Refusal to do a video call
  • Disrespectful or sexual messages you did not ask for
Report any concern by WhatsApp. We act on every report and remove members who break our rules. In an emergency, call the Ghana Police on 191 or the National Emergency number 112.
